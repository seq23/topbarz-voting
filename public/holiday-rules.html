Official Rules (DRAFT)

Top Barz Holiday Jingle Contest, at Dick & Jane’s, in partnership with Flow Music and Blitz & Bubbles.

1. Contest overview and administrator

Top Barz Inc. (“Top Barz”) runs the Top Barz Holiday Jingle Contest (the “Contest”) for eligible participants who record a song at the Top Barz booth at the holiday party hosted at the partner bar on Sunday, December 13, 2026 (the “Event”). Participants may enter an eligible song in a public vote on the Top Barz voting website. The partner bar is identified as the venue; these rules do not say that it administers the Contest unless the final rules name it as a sponsor.

2. Eligibility

The Contest is open to people who are 21 years of age or older on the date of the Event and who record at the Top Barz booth at the Event. Only songs recorded at the Top Barz booth at the Event are eligible: edited, re-recorded or other songs are not. Employees of Top Barz and of the partner bar, and members of their immediate families, are not eligible to win.

No purchase is necessary to enter or to win. A purchase does not improve the chances of winning. Entry and voting are free.

3. How to enter

Each participant signs up at the Event on their own with the QR code sign, giving their own name, phone number, email, city and optional Instagram handle. The Top Barz team marks who goes into the booth for each recording and who comes out, and the song is uploaded for exactly those participants. The participants open it at voting.topbarz.xyz/track with the 4-digit code the engineer gives them and their email; anyone with the code can hear and download the song and see who is on it.

Every participant on a song (everyone the Top Barz team marked into the booth for that recording, whether together or one after another) may add its artwork, set its title and enter or withdraw it, for everyone on it. To enter, a participant on the song ticks the “Enter the contest” box, which records who agreed to these rules and when, and the song goes into the public vote. [Open: whether the participant who enters a song agrees for everyone on it, and what waiver that needs, is still being decided. Until then this entry flow runs on the preview only.] Any participant on a song may withdraw it before voting ends by unticking the box, and the song then leaves the public vote; votes already cast are kept on record, and count again if the song is entered again.

Each participant must have the authority to grant the rights these rules require for their contribution. Songs that infringe anyone’s rights, or that contain hateful, threatening or unlawful material, may be removed from the Contest.

4. Contest period and voting

Voting takes place on the Top Barz voting website during the voting window. Voting closes Friday, November 27, 2026 at 11:59 PM Eastern Time. [Open: the time voting opens is to be confirmed in the final rules.] Only votes accepted within the window count; the website’s server records, not a voter’s device clock, control the cutoff.

Voters give their name, city and email address and enter the 6-digit code emailed to them; a vote counts only once that code is entered. One vote per email per song. A like is a vote; plays, comments and shares are not. No automated, purchased, fabricated or manipulated votes are permitted, and multiple email addresses may not be used to get around the voting limit. Top Barz may exclude votes shown to break these rules.

5. Winner selection and notification

Subject to eligibility, consent and review of valid votes, the eligible song with the most valid votes when voting closes is the potential winner. Public counts are provisional until the final tally and eligibility review are done. The prize is not awarded by random drawing. Top Barz contacts the potential winner by the phone number or email given at sign-up.

6. Prize

Prize details TBD. The prize, its approximate retail value and how it is redeemed are to be confirmed in the final rules. No cash alternative is offered unless the final rules say so. A winning group shares one prize and names one person to claim it. Winners should email info@topbarz.xyz.

7. Ownership and use of the songs

Participants keep ownership of their own lyrics and performance. Entering the Contest lets Top Barz show and play the entered song on the voting website and share it for unpaid promotion of the booth and the Contest. It does not give anyone the right to sell or monetize the song.

8. Privacy

Sign-up details are used to run the line at the booth, to text participants when it is their turn, to run the Contest and to contact the winner. Phone numbers and email addresses are never shown publicly; the website shows first names only. See the privacy note at voting.topbarz.xyz/privacy.

9. General

Top Barz may cancel, suspend or change the Contest if it cannot run as planned, to the extent the law allows. The Contest is void where prohibited. By ticking the box, each participant agrees to these rules.
