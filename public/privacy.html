Privacy note

This page is the Top Barz track vote for CultureCon. Here is what it collects when you vote, and why.

What we ask for

  • Your name, email and city, the first time you like, comment or share.
  • Whether you ticked “Send me Top Barz updates”. It starts unticked.

What we use it for

  • To count your vote: one like per track for each email.
  • To send contest results, and to reach the winner.
  • To send Top Barz updates, only if you ticked the box.
  • To spot and remove unfair voting.

What other people can see

  • Your first name, next to a comment you post. Nothing else.
  • Your email and city are never shown on the page. The Top Barz team sees them.

What else is kept

  • Your likes and comments, each with the time it was made.
  • A scrambled form of your network address, to limit repeat sign-ups. The address itself is not stored.
  • In your browser: a sign-in token and your first name, so you fill in the form once. Not your email.

Other services

  • The page is served by Cloudflare.
  • GIFs come from GIPHY. A GIF search is sent to GIPHY, and a GIF in a comment loads from GIPHY’s servers.

Questions, or want your details removed?

Message @topbarz.xyz on Instagram. Removing your details also removes your likes and comments.
