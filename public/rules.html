JUMP IN THE BOOTH

Official Rules

Top Barz CultureCon Song Contest. These are the working rules; the final public version follows for go-live.

1. Contest overview and administrator

Top Barz Inc. (“Top Barz”) facilitates the Top Barz CultureCon Song Contest (the “Contest”) for eligible participants who recorded a song at the Top Barz Studio Experience activation during CultureCon 2026. Participants may opt in to have an eligible track featured on the Top Barz website for public voting. The prize is one two-hour session at Studio 404 for the verified winning entry, subject to these rules and applicable studio policies.

Studio 404 LLC is the prize provider. CultureCon is identified as the event where the recordings were created; these rules do not represent that CultureCon administers or sponsors the Contest.

Studio 404 LLC, 593A Central Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11207. Studio 404 contact details are at studio404.nyc/contact.

2. Eligibility and entry

Entry is limited to people who recorded at the 2026 CultureCon Top Barz activation and accepted the applicable guest recording terms. Only tracks recorded at the Top Barz Studio Experience at CultureCon are eligible: edited, re-recorded or any other tracks are not eligible. The uploaded file must be the exact track that appears on the voting platform; do not swap in another track or edit the uploaded file.

Only participants who individually opt in through the official form may take part in the Contest or have their recording featured as contest content on the Top Barz website for public voting. Participation in the activation alone does not guarantee entry, public display or a prize. Each person must have authority to grant the rights required for their contribution.

For a track with more than one participant, each attached participant must consent to public-vote entry before the track appears in the Contest. One person’s consent does not authorize contest entry on behalf of another adult. Permission to share a track and consent to enter it in the public vote are separate choices.

To enter, a participant must complete the official opt-in form at voting.topbarz.xyz/entry and accept these rules. The form collects first name, last name, city, email address, phone number and Instagram handle(s); asks whether the participant recorded in a group; and provides a track-upload field and an optional track title. If the participant recorded in a group, the form collects the first name, last name and email address of every group member. Each group member must opt in individually. A group track is eligible only after every performer on it has opted in.

For a winning group track, the group must designate one contact to claim and coordinate the single prize. All winning-track participants and guests count toward the six-person limit. Studio 404 will act as the booking contact after Top Barz identifies the potential winner.

Entries must be booth-created recordings with cleared rights and complete consent. Test, demonstration and other non-contest tracks are not eligible. No separate entry fee is proposed.

3. Contest period and website

Voting will take place on the Top Barz voting website, starting Sunday, October 11, 2026, at 10:00 a.m. Eastern Time. Only votes accepted within the voting window count. The administrator’s server records, rather than a voter’s device clock, control the voting cutoff.

4. Voting, comments and sharing

Voters must enter an email address to cast a vote. A like is the vote mechanism; song plays, comments and shares are not votes. Voters may listen to eligible tracks, comment on them and share links across the internet, subject to the noncommercial-use restrictions below and any posted website conduct rules.

No automated, purchased, fabricated or manipulated votes are permitted. Multiple email addresses may not be used to evade the approved voting limit. Top Barz may review suspected abuse and exclude votes shown to violate the published rules; popularity or ordinary sharing alone is not evidence of fraud.

Comments must not infringe rights, impersonate others, harass, threaten or disclose private information. Top Barz may moderate comments and remove material that violates the posted rules or applicable law. Sharing a link does not transfer song ownership or grant a commercial-use license.

5. Winner selection and notification

Subject to eligibility, consent and valid-vote verification, the eligible track with the highest number of valid votes at the close of voting will be the potential winning track. Public counts remain provisional until the final tally and eligibility review are complete. The prize is not awarded by random drawing.

The potential winner becomes the confirmed winner only after the required review. No additional prize or payment is promised for other entrants or for ties. No unpublished tie-break or replacement-winner rule should be introduced after voting begins.

6. Prize and redemption

The prize is 2 (two) free hours of studio time at Studio 404, 593A Central Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11207, and 1 (one) general admission ticket to CultureCon 2027, provided by Top Barz Inc., for the winner or winning group associated with the winning entry. Winners should email info@topbarz.xyz.

A potential winner must claim the prize within seventy-two (72) hours after notification by contacting Top Barz and providing proof of the win. If the potential winner does not claim within 72 hours, Top Barz will proceed to the next potential winner in the final vote tally, subject to eligibility and valid-vote review. After confirmation, Top Barz will connect the winner or the winning group’s designated contact with Studio 404 by email; Studio 404 will coordinate booking directly with them. The session must take place by December 31, 2027.

Dates, times and room depend on availability. Studio 404 will coordinate with the winner and consider their preference. If Studio 404 needs to reschedule, it will agree a replacement date and time with the winner.

Attendance is limited to six (6) people total, including all winning-entry performers and guests, but excluding Studio 404 staff. The winner or designated group contact must provide Studio 404 a guest list with each attendee’s name and age at least 24 hours before the session. A guest change must be communicated at least one hour before the session. If a change is made with less than 24 hours’ notice, Studio 404 may rebook the session for a more appropriate time. Winners and guests must be in good standing with Studio 404; the studio may turn away a person it determines is not in good standing.

Travel to and from Studio 404 is not included. Any use beyond the two-hour session or additional expenses requires separate agreement.

An engineer and available equipment are included, equipment use is guided and supervised by studio staff, setup time depends on the equipment selected, and there is no limit on tracks worked on during the session. The specific engineer may be requested but is subject to availability. File delivery is normally during the session; Studio 404 may send files afterward if needed.

Lateness reduces the remaining session time; the winner should notify Studio 404 as soon as possible. A no-call/no-show forfeits the session.

7. Food and recording material

Winners and guests may bring food and are responsible for cleanup. Damage to furniture, equipment or other property is charged at cost. Safety staff may end a session for an imminent threat or danger.

The winner may use a beat they own or have permission to use. Studio 404 may help find an available beat, ask a producer about a beat, or make one during the session, but is not promising a particular beat or free use of a beat its producer intends to sell or license. Winners should communicate beat needs before the session so the studio can plan. Use of third-party beats or samples remains subject to the rights holder’s terms; a CultureCon booth beat is not automatically cleared for the prize session.

A prize session does not automatically include a license to reuse any CultureCon booth beat. The winner may not bring uncleared samples, unauthorized recordings or other material they lack permission to use.

8. Ownership and permitted use of CultureCon tracks

The “Top Barz x CultureCon - Guest Recording Terms” govern the booth-created track and the rights granted during the activation. These rules supplement those terms for contest participation; they do not enlarge the recording, composition, performance or publicity rights already granted.

Participants retain ownership of their original lyrics and performance, including their vocal contributions. Producers retain ownership of their beats and underlying instrumental compositions. Top Barz owns the final mix and master sound recording created at the booth, without acquiring ownership of participants’ underlying lyrics or performance or the producers’ underlying beats.

The booth beats were produced by Albert Akaya and Chadwick Mclaughlin (“Astro”). Their contributions remain subject to the applicable producer licenses; the Contest does not grant anyone independent ownership of or reuse rights in those beats.

No entrant, voter, guest, Top Barz, Studio 404 or other person receives permission under these rules to monetize a booth track. Prohibited uses include sales, paid downloads, streaming revenue, creator payouts attributable to the track, commercial licensing, sponsored content and advertising. The guest recording terms also prohibit uploading booth tracks to music-streaming or distribution services, including SoundCloud, even without revenue. Personal noncommercial social sharing is permitted within the accepted guest terms; extracting or redistributing a beat is not.

Top Barz’s use of booth tracks is limited to the purposes licensed in the guest terms, including delivery, the voting contest and unpaid activation/booth promotion. Paid ads and unrelated commercial uses require separate permission and all necessary clearances. These rules create no exception to those limits.

9. Rights in the prize-session recording

The prize-session recording is distinct from the CultureCon booth-created track. Production by Studio 404 engineers/producers is subject to a written agreement between the client and the studio, to be settled before recording. Third-party beat/sample rights remain with their owners. Top Barz’s booth-master ownership does not extend to the prize-session recording.

The winner and guests are responsible for their contributions and for obtaining permissions needed for later distribution or use. Top Barz does not guarantee that a new recording will be cleared for release or free of third-party claims.

10. Studio guidelines, safety and removal

The winner and each guest must follow Studio 404 policies, staff guidance, occupancy limits and applicable law. Smoking is prohibited in the studios. Studio 404 may stop a session when staff believe there is an imminent threat, danger or risk to safety or well-being.

Anyone under 18 attending the prize session must have a parent or guardian present for the full time they are at Studio 404.

To the extent permitted by applicable law, Top Barz is not responsible for Studio 404’s admission, removal or session-management decisions, participant or guest misconduct, or claims arising from a participant’s later creation, distribution or use of the prize-session song. Studio 404 remains responsible for its own legal obligations; these rules do not excuse obligations or liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.

11. Filming and social-media marketing

Top Barz and Studio 404 may ask to film or photograph the prize session for recap or social content. The winner and any filmed guest must give separate consent before promotional use; consent does not transfer music rights.

Filming permission does not transfer underlying music ownership or authorize monetization of the booth song. A winner cannot grant publicity or music rights owned by a friend, co-performer, producer or other rights holder.

12. Privacy and contest administration

Entrant and voter information is used for contest entry, voting administration, verification, winner notification and prize fulfillment. Voter email addresses are not displayed publicly. Marketing enrollment is a separate, optional choice, not inferred from voting or entry.

13. Acceptance

Entry requires affirmative acceptance of these Official Rules. Prize acceptance is subject to eligibility verification and the final studio terms. Each attending adult must accept applicable studio and filming terms personally; any legally required guardian consent must be verified separately.
